Your markup. Your theme.

Folio uses all thirteen Graupl core components, plus its buttons, links, forms, tables, typography, and layout utilities. Icons and navigation shelf are not included.

Keep theme.css after Graupl's stylesheet. Every declaration in the theme sets an existing --graupl-* property. Most declarations are on :root. Scoped properties customize buttons, badges, cards, and accordion headers. The only custom script controls appearance, not components.

Remove the theme stylesheet to use base Graupl with the same markup and interactions.
