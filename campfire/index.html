04 / INTERACTION

Around the campground

Graupl's generators handle every interaction below.

Before you arrive

Pick up your site number and a campground map at the camp office.

Keep voices low and turn off speakers after 10 p.m.

The drinking water tap is beside site 12 on Hemlock Loop.

Campsite notes

Hemlock Loop

Pitch your tent inside the marked pad. The water tap is beside site 12.

Lakeside Loop

Take the footpath to the dock. Leave the shore clear for people launching canoes.

Fern Loop

Follow the short trail through the hemlocks. It returns to the camp office.

Camp essentials

Keep food, scented items, and rubbish in the storage lockers overnight.

Your markup. Your theme.

Campfire uses all thirteen Graupl core components, plus its buttons, links, forms, tables, typography, and layout utilities. Icons and navigation shelf are not included.

Keep theme.css after Graupl's stylesheet. Existing --graupl-* properties still define the theme, mostly on :root. Scoped styles and decorative pseudo-elements add component details. The only custom script controls appearance, not components.

Remove the theme stylesheet to use base Graupl with the same markup and interactions.
